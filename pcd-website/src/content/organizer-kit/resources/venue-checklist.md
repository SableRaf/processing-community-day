---
title: Venue Checklist
section: Resources
order: 2
description: Questions to ask about a space before you commit to it.
---

- [ ] Can you use the space for free or is there a fee?
- [ ] Confirm availability for the date.
- [ ] Is the internet connection reliable for your group size?
- [ ] Is there a projector, screen, or sound system?
- [ ] Are there enough power outlets? Do you need extension cords?
- [ ] Are acoustics and noise levels suitable for talks and discussions?
- [ ] Is the venue easy to reach by public transport?
- [ ] Is the space accessible for disabled participants?
- [ ] Are there gender-neutral bathrooms?
- [ ] Is the space suitable for children, if relevant?
- [ ] Is there enough room for your planned activities, including parallel sessions?
- [ ] Can people move easily between areas?
- [ ] Is the venue easy to find once inside the building? Is there space for a welcome desk?
- [ ] Will someone from the venue be there to unlock doors, support, or troubleshoot?
- [ ] Are emergency exits clearly marked?
- [ ] Is basic first aid available?
