title: Accessibility
section: Event Resources
order: 5
description: Making your PCD welcoming and usable for disabled participants.
---

Pay attention to the infrastructure and amenities of the space. Is the space accessible for disabled participants? Are there gender-neutral bathrooms? Is the venue easy to reach by public transport? See the [Venue Checklist](/organize/resources/venue-checklist/) for a detailed list.

We recommend this accessibility checklist: [https://accessibility.cornell.edu/event-planning/accessible-meeting-and-event-checklist/](https://accessibility.cornell.edu/event-planning/accessible-meeting-and-event-checklist/)

Publish accessibility information on your event page so participants can decide in advance whether the event works for them.
