Join an evening of creative experimentation developed by the V&A and arebyte Digital Art Centre.

As part of Processing Community Day, the evening will feature a community Show & Tell celebrating creative coding, digital art and interactive design. Want to share a project to be presented? Email us at digitalprogrammes@vam.ac.uk to be considered for the Show & Tell.

Here's our amazing line up: 

- Ambie Drew
- Olivia Ema
- Emir Johnston
- Freddie Sanders
- Maria Than
- + show & tell projects.

Meet artists and creative technologists, discover inspiring projects, learn from their practice, and exchange ideas with a community of creatives, makers and technologists.
